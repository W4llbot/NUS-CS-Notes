“social masking” - immense cognitive effort required by individuals with autism or  social anxiety to navigate the invisible rules and implicit demands of society
AI companions create a safe, judgment-free relational space for “unmasking”

Arguments in the video appear biased and one-sided

### Logic
Video takes liberties to make immense logical leaps
Implication that heavy AI use directly causes people with "no prior history of mental illness" to suddenly experience psychosis
Problematic on 2 fronts
1. Correlation does not equal causation
Anecdotal evidence of people citing LLMs as a contributing factor to psychological experiences does not mean that the technology caused it
2. There is no evidence that AI use can “induce” psychosis on healthy individuals
AI-associated delusions and large language models: risks, mechanisms of delusion co-creation, and safeguarding strategies 
Study shows that this phenomena is only consistently observed in subjects with pre-existing vulnerability to psychosis [1]

Provides unsubstantiated arguments that look convincing to the general public
Induces unnecessary fear that potentially incentivises legislation or regulation on a technology that can have a positive effect on society

# Tutorial 1-3 Summary
Affordances: relationship between the properties of an object and the capabilities of the agent that determine how the object could possibly be used

## Affordances of digital tools
- multimodality - combines text, image, audio, video
- interactivity - user engagement
    - spreadability
    - searchability
- intertextuality/re-contextualization - linking/repurposing texts in new contexts
- collaboration
- identity construction

## Paul and Elders critical thinking framework
Applying ***intellectual standards*** to many ***elements of thought*** allows us to develop beneficial ***intellectual traits***
- elements + standards --> traits

### The 8 Elements of thought
Essential dimension of reasoning that are present whenever reasoning occurs
- purpose
- questions
- POVs
- information
- concepts
- inferences/conclusion
- assumptions
- implications/consequences

### The 10 intellectual standards
Evaluates the ***quality*** of the elements of thought
- clarity
- accuracy
- precision
- relevance
- depth
- breadth
- logic
- significance
- fairness
- sufficiency

### The 8 intellectual traits
Developed by applying aforementioned intellectual standards to thought process
- humility
- autonomy
- integrity
- courage
- perseverance
- confidence in reason
- empathy
- fair-mindedness

# Tutorial 6
## Commentary text structure
Intro
1. title
2. hook
    - choose one
        - anecdote or story?
        - vivid imagery or scenario?
        - provocative question?
        - contrast or irony?
        - striking fact, news or statistic?
    - state your case
        - clearly present central argument or thesis early (maybe even in title)
        - be straight forward and direct
        - show your angle and make it arguable
3. context
    - set the stage - a general statement summarising the current situation
    - provide background information
4. urgency & relevance
5. thesis

Body
1. argument
    1. claim
    2. reasoning
    3. evidence
2. argument
    1. claim
    2. reasoning
    3. evidence

Conclusion
1. tail
    1. action
    2. thought
2. end
    1. concluding remark
    2. one powerful sentence

## creating novelty in commentary
De-familiarization - question fundamental concepts
Interdisciplinary questioning - reframe topic through the perspective of another discipline to generate new insight
Debunking myth - challenge misleading beliefs to open new pathways of thinking
